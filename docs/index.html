The rules — what adds itself, what waits for you

Two of the five inclusion buckets are size-gated formalities, so the lane applies them without asking. The other three are judgement calls and always queue. An undecided rule defaults to the status quo: wrongly queueing costs one Slack reply, wrongly auto-adding puts a row in the fleet's most-depended-on artifact.

BucketRuleThresholdBehaviourWhy
2Any IPO or direct listing, any sector ≥ $25BAUTO Mandatory by rule — queueing it just puts a step between the rule and the outcome.
3Spin-off, carve-out, separation > $10BAUTO Same. Applies at separation, never before — no shares trade, so there is no cap to gate on.
1Core-sector IPO / listing / spin-off — HC services, MedTech, tools, diagnostics, HCIT, adjacent tech any sizeQUEUES No size floor at all, so auto-adding would sweep in every microcap biotech that lists.
4Strategically relevant new candidate $2–20BQUEUES “Strategically relevant” is explicitly a judgement call. Not mechanisable.
5Russell 1000 / 2000 first-time addition, any sector $2–20BQUEUES ~40 a quarter, mostly unfamiliar names.

Three refusals that override everything above

  • No market cap means no auto-add, ever. Both auto buckets are size-gated, and unknown is not qualifying. This matters most for spin-offs — a Form 10 candidate legitimately has no cap until it trades.
  • A ticker on the provenance removals list is never auto-added. A vendor has no idea a row was deliberately removed and will keep re-proposing it. An FMP biopharma screen re-proposed ALBT eight days after it was removed for leaving healthcare.
  • Auto means unasked, not unvalidated. Every auto-add routes through the same enrichment gate as a manual one, which refuses a half-filled row and says why.

Company Summaries

Zhongji Innolight Co., Ltd. (3308.HK)

We design and manufacture the high-speed optical transceivers that move data between the GPUs, servers and switches inside AI data centers, with a portfolio running from 10G to 1.6T. We sell modules directly to hyperscalers and networking OEMs — Nvidia, Google, Meta and Huawei among them — and we make money on volume manufacturing at scale, where being first to qualify each new speed generation earns us the highest margins before competitors catch up. In 2025 we generated RMB 38.24B (~US$5.6B) of revenue, up 60% year over year, with net profit up 109%; Q1 2026 revenue was RMB 19.5B, up 192% year over year, at a record 46% gross margin. We are currently the only supplier able to mass-produce both 800G and 1.6T modules, which is why we hold over half of Nvidia's optical-module spend.

Jersey Mike's Subs Inc. (JMKE)

We franchise a chain of more than 3,300 submarine-sandwich shops across all 50 US states and Canada, 99% of which are owned and operated by franchisees rather than by us. We make money primarily by collecting royalties and fees on our franchisees' sales plus margin on the food and supplies we distribute to them, which means our revenue scales with systemwide sales without our funding the stores. In 2025 our franchisees rang up $4.3B of systemwide sales, up 13%, on which we recognised $724M of revenue, $55M of net income and $327–339M of adjusted EBITDA — roughly a 47% margin. Average unit volume is about $1.4M, franchisee cash-on-cash returns run near 42%, and over 1,600 committed new stores are in the pipeline with more than 90% being built by operators who already own one of ours.

Latigo Biotherapeutics, Inc. (LTGO)

We are a clinical-stage pharmaceutical company developing non-opioid pain medicines that block pain signals at the peripheral nerve rather than in the brain, so they relieve pain without the addiction and sedation risk that define opioids. Our approach is to selectively inhibit Nav1.8, a sodium channel expressed almost exclusively on pain-sensing neurons — our lead candidate LTG-001 is an oral inhibitor for acute pain with Phase 2 results published in the New England Journal of Medicine, and LTG-305 is a follow-on in Phase 1 aimed at chronic pain. We have no product revenue and fund ourselves from equity: roughly $321.5M raised privately since 2018, including a $150M Series B led by Blue Owl Capital in March 2025 with Sanofi Ventures, Qatar Investment Authority and Cormorant participating, plus $345.6M gross from this offering. We will make money by selling a branded oral analgesic into the acute-pain market that Vertex's Journavx opened commercially.

Fabrinet (FN)

We are the contract manufacturer the optical-networking industry outsources its hardest hardware to: we build the transceivers, lasers and photonic assemblies that our customers design but do not want to make themselves, mostly out of large campuses in Thailand. We make money on manufacturing services — our customers own the designs and the end-customer relationships, and we earn a margin on precision assembly and test that requires capital and process expertise they would rather not carry. Optical communications is 73.2% of our revenue, and in our fiscal Q3 2026 we delivered a record $1.214B of revenue, up 39% year over year, with data-center interconnect alone up 90.4% to $196.9M. We have launched two datacom transceiver programs with a hyperscale customer and several merchant transceiver programs for data-center scale-out that begin production in early fiscal 2027.
