Three refusals that override everything above

  • No market cap means no auto-add, ever. The size buckets are gated on it, and for Bucket 1 — which has no floor — it stands in for “does this actually trade yet”. A Form 10 SpinCo has a core SIC code and no shares, so it cannot auto-add on the sector alone. A cap written as prose (~$3.7B) is refused too, and says so rather than claiming the cap is missing.
  • A ticker on the provenance removals list is never auto-added. A vendor has no idea a row was deliberately removed and will keep re-proposing it. An FMP biopharma screen re-proposed ALBT eight days after it was removed for leaving healthcare.
  • Auto means unasked, not unvalidated. Every auto-add routes through the same enrichment gate as a manual one, which refuses a half-filled row and says why.

Company Summaries

Jio Platforms Limited (JPL.NS)

We are India's largest mobile operator, with ~533 million subscribers paying ~₹214 ($2.25) a month on mostly prepaid plans. We also run the country's largest home-broadband business (JioFiber / AirFiber). We earned FY26 EBITDA of ₹76,255cr on ₹146,885cr of revenue (~52% margin) and ₹30,049cr of net profit. Reliance controls us; Meta and Google are minority holders.

Lycia Therapeutics, Inc. (LYCA)

We design drugs that pull disease-causing proteins out of the bloodstream and route them to the liver to be destroyed, an extracellular approach built on Carolyn Bertozzi's LYTAC work. Our lead, LCA-0061, clears IgE in allergic disease (Phase 1), and LCA-0321 targets Graves' disease autoantibodies. Data for both are expected in 2027. We have no product revenue.

Bambusa Therapeutics, Inc. (BBTX)

We develop bispecific antibodies for inflammatory disease. Our lead, BBT001, hits IL-4Rα (the Dupixent target) and IL-31 (itch) in one molecule for atopic dermatitis, and showed a placebo-adjusted EASI benefit from week 1 in a July 2026 proof-of-concept study. We bought the asset from BioNTech's Chinese subsidiary. We are pre-revenue, lost $41.1M in 2025, and say the IPO funds us into late 2028.

Aspen Neuroscience, Inc. (ASPE)

We turn a Parkinson's patient's own skin cells into dopamine neurons and transplant them back into the brain. Because the cells are the patient's own, the therapy should need no immunosuppression. Sasineprocel is in the Phase 1/2a ASPIRO trial. We lost $62.1M in 2025, and our S-1 says the IPO money will not carry us through Phase 3.

JIO PLATFORMS LIMITED (JPL.NS) — Quick Background

Sector / Industry: Tech / Telecom & Digital Services (Indian wireless + home broadband) Market Cap: ~₹13.2 lakh crore ≈ $139B (FMP ₹1,431 on 2026-10-30, at 94.94 INR/USD) | Index: NSE/BSE since 10-28 Closest comps: Bharti Airtel (not covered), META, NFLX, SPOT M&A FLAG: None at Jio itself. Reliance Industries remains the controlling shareholder after the IPO. The ₹30,213cr fresh issue was primary capital.

1. Business Description

We are India's largest mobile operator. At the end of June 2026 we had 533 million subscribers, 268 million of them on 5G as of March. Almost all of them buy prepaid monthly plans, and our average revenue per user is ₹214 a month, about $2.25. It was ₹182 in FY24, and most of the rise came from industry-wide tariff increases rather than from customers buying more. That is the business: a very large number of people each paying very little, on a network we built from scratch after launching in 2016 with free data, which pushed the industry from roughly a dozen operators down to three private ones.

Our second business is home and enterprise connectivity: JioFiber, the fixed-wireless JioAirFiber product, and enterprise connectivity and cloud. It is smaller, but it is where our subscriber growth has been fastest. We also hold digital-services assets: music (JioSaavn), devices and an app ecosystem. Our video assets sit partly within the wider Reliance group, so a reader should not assume the streaming businesses are all inside this entity.

Profitability is our differentiator against the rest of Indian telecom. We earned FY26 EBITDA of ₹76,255cr on ₹146,885cr of revenue, a ~52% margin, and ₹30,049cr of net profit. Bharti is our only scaled rival. Vodafone Idea is financially weak, so pricing is now set by a two-player market. That structure is the investment case. Meta and Google bought minority stakes in 2020.

LYCIA THERAPEUTICS, INC. (LYCA) — Quick Background

Sector / Industry: Biopharma / Biotech — extracellular targeted protein degradation Market Cap: ~$1.44B at $27.73 (FMP, 2026-10-30; +54% vs the $18 offer) | Index: Nasdaq since 10-23 Closest comps: KYMR, ARVN, GLUE, NRIX (degraders); JSPR (allergy/mast cell) M&A FLAG: None

1. Business Description

We make drugs that remove harmful proteins from outside the cell, which the intracellular degraders (Kymera, Arvinas) cannot reach. Our molecules bind a circulating target, such as IgE or an autoantibody, and tag it for uptake and destruction by liver receptors. The approach comes from Nobel laureate Carolyn Bertozzi's LYTAC research at Stanford, and she co-founded the company. Antibodies such as Xolair block IgE; we aim to eliminate it, which in principle gives deeper suppression and less frequent dosing.

We have three named programs. LCA-0061, the lead, is an antibody–small-molecule conjugate against IgE and is in Phase 1 in people with elevated IgE, including food-allergy patients. LCA-0062 is a fast-follower IgE degrader in IND-enabling work. LCA-0321 targets TSH-receptor autoantibodies in Graves' disease and is in Phase 1. Data from LCA-0061 and LCA-0321 are expected in 2027, and initial LCA-0062 data in 2H27.

We have no product revenue. We earned collaboration revenue from a 2021 Eli Lilly deal ($35M upfront), which ran off to zero in 1H26. We raised $267M of venture capital, led by Versant (~19% pre-IPO), and now ~$318M in the IPO. With $138M of cash at 09-30-26, we guide our runway into 2H29.

1. Business Description

ASPEN NEUROSCIENCE, INC. (ASPE) — Quick Background

Sector / Industry: Biopharma / Biotech — autologous iPSC-derived cell therapy, Parkinson's disease Market Cap: ~$0.44B at the $15 IPO price (Est., ~29.1M shares) | Index: Nasdaq, first trade 10-30 Closest comps: BlueRock (Bayer, private), SANA, CRBU; Parkinson's: ABBV (tavapadon), PRAX M&A FLAG: None

1. Business Description

We make a personalised cell therapy for Parkinson's disease. We take a skin biopsy from the patient, reprogram the cells into induced pluripotent stem cells, grow them into the dopamine-producing neurons the disease destroys, and implant them into the patient's brain. Because the cells are the patient's own, we expect no need for lifelong immunosuppression. That is the main mechanical difference from the off-the-shelf approach Bayer's BlueRock is taking with bemdaneprocel. Our lead, sasineprocel, is in the Phase 1/2a ASPIRO trial in mid-stage, "moderately advanced" Parkinson's.

We are pre-revenue and spend about $50–60M a year: a net loss of $62.1M in 2025 and $36.5M in 1H26, with $72.4M of cash at 6/30/26. Our own S-1 says the IPO money plus existing cash will not be enough to finish ASPIRO or the planned Phase 3, so we will need to raise again. The deal priced at the bottom of the $15–17 range for $140.6M gross.
